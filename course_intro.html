Getting started

Who do I contact if I need help?

What do I need to start the module?

You need a computer or tablet (iPad) with internet access. It is not recommended to use a mobile phone to view the course. The device needs to have a speaker that can play audio.

Note: Videos are included throughout this course as a supplementary learning tool. If a video does not load due to slow internet, the written content on each page covers the same information, so you will not miss anything. Some videos are provided for learners who wish to explore a topic further and are not required to complete the module.

Do I need an email and password to log in to the module?

No, you do not need to log in to access the module.

Time and progress

How long is the module?

The module consists of an introduction, 3 lessons, and a summary. There will be a short quiz after each lesson and a final assessment at the end that covers all course content. It should take approximately 1 hour to finish.

How much time do I have to finish the module?

The module is self-paced, so you can finish at your own pace. If you decide to stop and return to the module later, please take note of where you left off.

This learning module is part of a student capstone research project. To keep on schedule with deadlines, please finish the module within 2 weeks.

How can I pick up where I left off?

Because the learning module does not require a login to an account, it will not save your progress. It is highly recommended to write down or type your text responses in Microsoft Word, and to record which lesson you left off on.

How the course works

How is the module designed?

The module consists of four main components:

  • a preliminary survey,
  • the learning content,
  • the final assessment,
  • and the post module survey.

The preliminary survey is designed to assess your current knowledge about online safety practices. Once you've completed the learning content and final assessment, your preliminary survey results will be compared to your post module survey results to measure the effectiveness of the learning material. Final assessment scores will also be used to determine whether learning objectives were met.

What is the learning content like?

The module content is made up of an introduction, 3 lessons, and a summary.

The course begins with a scenario for you to think about how you would respond if you found yourself in a similar situation. It is relevant for all 3 lessons. After working through the course content, you will return to the scenario and select the best course of action. The correct answer will then be revealed so you can compare it with your own response and reflect on what you've learned.

At the end of each lesson, you will complete a short quiz to check your learning. The results do not count toward your final score.

Will my assessment be graded?

Yes, all assessments will be scored. All scores will be kept confidential and are not connected to your name or identifying information.

Is there a way to download the course content?

Yes! There will be a page at the end of the course where you can download a PDF version of the course text. You can save it and print it out for studying later.

Technical issues

Why do I see a "Redirect notice" when clicking links?

This is a safety feature put in place by Google to protect you from visiting suspicious links. If you receive a "Redirect notice" page when clicking on a link within the course, don't worry. The same link will appear again on the page. Click that link to visit the webpage.

Why doesn't the course load?

If you have a slow internet connection, the pages of the course may take longer to load. Please allow it a bit of time, and if it still doesn't work, hit refresh to reload the page. If you are having problems viewing any part of the module, please contact Amanda by emailing her at awalke11@wgu.edu.
