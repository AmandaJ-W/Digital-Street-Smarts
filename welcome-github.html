Digital Street Smarts: Navigating Online Risks

Welcome, learner!

Welcome to Digital Street Smarts: Navigating Online Risks. Thank you for taking part in this capstone project. Your participation is greatly valued!

What to expect

  • About 1 hour

    A short learning module about staying safe online. You can go at your own pace.

  • Have notes ready

    Keep a pen and paper or a Microsoft Word document nearby for notes and scenario tasks.

  • No login needed

    You do not need an account or a password to take this course.

Your personal code

To protect your privacy, a personal code will be provided to you before taking the pre-assessment. Please write this code down or take a picture of it. You will be asked to enter it to take the pre-assessment, post-assessment, and post-module feedback survey. This code will be connected to your assessment and survey answers, but will not be used to identify you personally.

It is important that you write your code down or take a picture of it so you can use the same code each time.

Please use a computer for this course

This course is designed to be viewed on a desktop or laptop computer. A phone or tablet may not display the content correctly. If you are currently on a phone or tablet, it is recommended to switch to a computer before continuing. If you are unable to switch devices, please be aware that some content may display improperly.

Before you begin

This course WILL NOT SAVE YOUR PROGRESS. If you need to stop and come back later, please write down the name of the page where you left off.
